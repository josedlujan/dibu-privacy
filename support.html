Frequently asked questions

Where are my drawings saved?
Drawings are stored only on your device.

Does Dibu need an account or internet connection?
No. You can color without creating an account and without an internet connection.

Does the app include ads or purchases?
No. The current version does not include ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
