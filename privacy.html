Dibu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Dibu is a coloring app designed for children and families. We respect our users’ privacy.

Information We Collect

Dibu does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. It does not request names, email addresses, location, contacts, camera, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Saved Drawings

Your saved drawings and projects are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Dibu does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies.

Purchases

The current version of Dibu does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. If these are added in the future, they will be processed by Apple and this policy will be updated before release.

Children’s Privacy

Dibu is designed to provide an age-appropriate experience for children. Because we do not collect personal information or display advertising, we do not share children’s data with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if Dibu’s features or privacy practices change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact josedlujan@gmail.com.
